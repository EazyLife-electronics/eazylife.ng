# EazyLife — ISF Forum 2026 Pitch Deck Outline

This is the slide-by-slide content reference for the 10-slide ISF Forum 2026 presentation. The visual presentation should remain the approved deck; this outline is a text companion for editing, rehearsal and future updates.

## 01 — EazyLife Robotics & Electronics Solutions
**Headline:** Making technology more accessible.  
**Focus:** Robotics, electronics, automation and affordable practical STEM.  
**Positioning:** Early-stage Nigerian engineering company working towards practical solutions and locally developed products.

## 02 — The Problem
**Headline:** Technology is difficult to access, learn and localize.  
- Access: components, tools and technical guidance can be difficult to source.
- Practical learning: learners need affordable ways to experiment, build, test and understand.
- Local solutions: imported products may be difficult to adapt, repair or customize.
**Gap:** Connect component access with practical learning and engineering.

## 03 — Our Solution
**Headline:** One engineering company. Four connected capabilities.
1. Robotics & automation.
2. Electronics & hardware.
3. Affordable practical STEM.
4. Prototyping & a pathway towards production.

## 04 — What Exists Today
**Headline:** A working digital platform—and engineering ideas in development.
- EazyLife online platform.
- STEM Lab with 20 interactive projects.
- Ultrasonic servo radar simulation.
- Concept/design visuals must be labelled as concepts, not completed physical products.
**Stage clarity:** No dedicated workshop or established manufacturing capacity yet.

## 05 — Customers & Delivery
**Headline:** From a need to a working outcome.
**Customers:** Students and makers; schools and academies; engineers and researchers; small businesses.  
**Delivery path:** Discover → Define → Deliver → Improve.

## 06 — Business Model
**Headline:** Multiple ways to earn—one engineering ecosystem.
- Products and components.
- Engineering services and prototyping.
- Paid STEM programmes.
- Future sales of EazyLife-designed products.

## 07 — Why EazyLife
**Headline:** We connect what is usually separated.
Connect component access, practical learning, engineering support and a pathway from ideas to locally developed products. STEM supports the wider engineering and product-development mission.

## 08 — The $10,000 Opportunity
**Headline:** Build the first dedicated EazyLife engineering workshop.
- $2,500 workshop rent/setup/electrical installation.
- $2,000 engineering tools/equipment.
- $1,500 components/materials.
- $1,500 product development/testing.
- $1,000 digital platform/STEM Lab.
- $750 marketing/customer acquisition.
- $500 quality assurance/operations.
- $250 working capital.
**Total: $10,000.** First priority: secure and prepare the workspace.

## 09 — Where We're Going
**Headline:** By 2029: a Nigerian engineering company with products in the market.
Targets, not current achievements:
- 5+ products commercialized.
- 2,000 paying customers.
- 20 partnerships.
- 8–12 team members.
- Dedicated prototyping workshop and small-scale production.

## 10 — Closing
**Headline:** Let's make technology more accessible.
**Ask:** Capital, manufacturing partners, technical mentors, distributors and schools ready to build with EazyLife.  
**Closing message:** “We don't just want to be customers of the world's technology. We want to become creators, developers and producers of technology ourselves.”
