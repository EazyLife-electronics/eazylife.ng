LEARNING APPROACH

Understand it. Test it. Build it.

Lessons combine clear explanations, honest interactive models, practical build guidance and challenges. A simulation is labelled according to what it actually represents, not presented as a perfect hardware emulator.

Learn

Understand the principle, terminology and important relationships.

Experiment

Change variables, observe model behaviour and answer investigation prompts.

Build

Take suitable low-voltage activities to a real breadboard or workbench.
