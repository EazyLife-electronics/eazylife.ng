How to read the bands

  1. Band 1: first significant digit.
  2. Band 2: second significant digit.
  3. Band 3: multiplier.
  4. Band 4: tolerance.
Resistance = significant digits × multiplierThe tolerance band describes the allowed range around the nominal value.

Worked examples

  • Brown · Black · Red · Gold10 × 100 = 1,000 Ω ±5%
  • Red · Violet · Black · Blue · Brown270 × 1,000,000 = 270 MΩ ±1%

Start reading from the end where the bands are grouped closer together. The tolerance band is usually separated slightly from the others.

A missing tolerance band is commonly interpreted as ±20% on a 4-band resistor. This calculator does not support every specialised or non-standard resistor marking.

Real resistor markings can be faded, damaged or non-standard. When accuracy matters, measure the component with a digital multimeter instead of relying on colour identification alone.
